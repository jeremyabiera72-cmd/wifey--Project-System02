Fare formula used for every type:
trip fare = max(minimum fare, base fare + max(0, distance − included distance) × rate per km)
total = trip fare × passengers (per passenger) or total = trip fare (per booking), plus any additional charges. Distance comes from the map route, and the fare is always recalculated on the server before it is saved.
